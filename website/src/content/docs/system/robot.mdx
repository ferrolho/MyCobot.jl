## Where the interfaces are

The base is round, with a rectangular box at the back. In robot axes, +x is the
front: the arm points in that direction at the zero pose.

<BaseOrientation />

| Location | This site | Elephant's documentation | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back face of the box (−x) | back face | "front of the base", function interface groups 1–4 | 2.54 mm Arduino-style sockets and pins. Groups 1 and 4 follow the Arduino UNO/MEGA layout, groups 2 and 3 the MKR WiFi 1010 layout. The FT232R connects here. |
| Top face of the box | top face | "top of the base", function interface group 5 | Header strip with Arduino pin labels, and the 4-pin TRVG port. |
| −y side | robot's right | "right side of the base" | DC power jack (12 V; 6.5 mm outer, 2.0 mm inner) on the box, next to the round part. The rating label on the round part. |
| Arm end | ATOM socket | function interface group 6 | See [ATOM](/mycobot-280-lab/system/atom/#the-socket-in-the-arm) and [Gripper](/mycobot-280-lab/system/gripper/#mount-and-cable). |

The −y side is on your left when you face the robot. Photos of the base
(2026-10-04) agree with the URDF mesh and with Elephant's documentation.

## Back face: function interface groups 1–4

<BaseHeaders panel="ft232" />

Only GND and pins 13 and 14 are used.

<FtdiWiring />

| FT232R | Base |
| --- | --- |
| TXD | pin 14 |
| RXD | pin 13 |
| GND | GND |

The base passes the bytes on pins 13/14 to the servo bus, and the servo replies
back. The laptop does not receive its own transmissions. The servos also answer
when the ATOM is removed, so the base, not the ATOM, makes this bridge.

## Top face: function interface group 5 and the TRVG port

<BaseHeaders panel="trvg" />

The 4-pin **TRVG** port is probably TX, RX, V and GND. It is not yet known if it
carries the same serial line as pins 13/14.

:::note[Not yet measured]
The logic level on pins 13/14 (3.3 V or 5 V) is not measured. Measure the `IOR`
pin and pin 13 to GND before you connect a 3.3 V device such as an ESP32.
:::

## Joint limits

From the URDF (`mycobot_description/urdf/mycobot_280_arduino/`):

| Joint | J1 | J2 | J3 | J4 | J5 | J6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Limit (±°) | 168 | 135 | 150 | 145 | 165 | 180 |

The servos do not enforce these limits (their angle limit registers allow 0–4095).
The arm can rest outside them, for example when it is folded by hand while limp.
The players use a margin of 10°.

## Zero pose

At 0° on every joint the arm is upright, and the flange points forward (+x).
The flange is at (45.6, −64.6, 411.1) mm in the URDF base frame.
